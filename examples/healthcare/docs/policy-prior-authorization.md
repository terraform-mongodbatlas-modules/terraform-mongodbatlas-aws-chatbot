# Springfield Mutual Health — Prior Authorization (PA) Policy

Record type: Plan policy | FICTIONAL DEMO DATA

Some drugs and services are covered only after Springfield Mutual Health approves them in advance. All specialty-tier drugs need prior authorization.

- The prescriber submits the PA request with clinical documentation. Standard decisions take up to 15 days; urgent requests take 72 hours.
- Approvals are time-limited. Growth hormone (somatropin) approvals last 6 months and need renewal.
- Growth hormone renewal criteria: an endocrinologist's visit note from the last 90 days, a growth chart showing improved height velocity, a bone-age X-ray from the last 12 months, and an IGF-1 lab result.
- If the plan asks for more information, the request pauses until the prescriber sends it. If the approval expires before renewal, fills are not covered until the new approval.
- Members can request a one-time 30-day transition fill while a renewal is pending by calling Member Services at 1-555-SMH-HELP.
- Denials can be appealed within 180 days.
