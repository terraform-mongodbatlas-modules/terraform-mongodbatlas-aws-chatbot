# Springfield Mutual Health — Proxy Access Policy

Record type: Plan policy | FICTIONAL DEMO DATA

- Parents and legal guardians automatically get full proxy access to records of dependent children under age 13.
- Children ages 13–17 control access to confidential services (for example reproductive and behavioral health); parents keep access to everything else.
- Adult members (18 and older), including spouses on the same family plan, must grant proxy access themselves. Being on the same plan or at the same address does not give one adult access to another adult's clinical records.
- Without proxy access, another member can see only plan-level information: the adult's name on the member list, and their contribution to family deductible and out-of-pocket totals.
- An adult can grant access in the member portal under Settings > Proxy Access > Invite a family member, or by calling Member Services at 1-555-SMH-HELP.
- Customer service representatives and the virtual assistant follow the same rules and will not disclose a restricted member's visits, diagnoses, prescriptions, claims, or providers.
